### Sam Huffard

Washington and Lee '28, currently studying abroad. Congressional intern in the U.S. Senate, and chapter lead for the Third Serve Foundation at W&L.

I build small AI systems that do real work — a multi-agent research assistant that proposes trades against a live brokerage account, a personal assistant that manages my email and calendar, and an agent system built for a congressional office. The common thread: the AI proposes, a human (or a deterministic rule the AI can't override) decides, and every decision leaves a signed, auditable trail.

I use Claude as a full development partner — architecture, implementation, and code review — and treat prompting and context management as a real skill, not an afterthought.

## Projects

| Project | Summary |
|---|---|
| [portfolio-manager](https://github.com/samuelhuffard/portfolio-manager) | Three AI agents research a live brokerage account daily and queue buy/sell/hold proposals with sizing and a written thesis. No autonomous execution — every trade is a human-approved, cryptographically signed action, with a crash-safe execution flow and an append-only audit ledger. |
| [portfolio-dashboard](https://github.com/samuelhuffard/portfolio-dashboard) | The human side of Portfolio Manager — the approval queue, live holdings, and performance tracking against every past recommendation, not just the winners. |
| [aide-ai](https://github.com/samuelhuffard/aide-ai) | A federated multi-agent AI system built for a congressional office. Seven specialist agents draft constituent replies, bill briefings, and scheduling briefs — every outbound action requires staff approval. Public repo is a de-identified snapshot; identifying details have been generalized. |
| [mlb-power-rankings](https://github.com/samuelhuffard/mlb-power-rankings) | A weekly MLB power-rankings site with a custom scoring model (FIP, WAR, strength of schedule), backed by a Google Sheet instead of a database. |
